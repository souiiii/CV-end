# CV Endsem Notes — Agent Guidelines

## Goal

Create accurate, well-structured Computer Vision notes optimized for the end-semester examination. Prioritize exam relevance, clarity, and efficient preparation over comprehensive subject coverage.

## Source Priority

1. Teacher's instructions in `Syllabus/` are authoritative. Newer instructions override older ones.
2. Official syllabus defines supporting context.
3. Lecture slides in `Sources/` provide primary study material.
4. PYQs in `Papers/` help identify question styles and expected depth, but may contain irrelevant material from other branches.

Never expand the study scope solely because something appears in the syllabus, slides, or PYQs.

## Scope Control

- Follow the approved `EXAM_SCOPE.md` when available.
- Exclude explicitly ruled-out topics, including Vision Transformers and RNNs.
- Include prerequisites only when necessary to understand examinable material.
- When scope is ambiguous, flag the uncertainty instead of silently including additional topics.

## Note-Writing Standards

- Use a clear, prominent heading for each topic.
- Start with **what it is**, followed by the important concepts, explanations, and working principles.
- Include formulas, diagrams, examples, and exam-style questions wherever they genuinely improve understanding or are relevant to the exam.
- Explain concepts in complete, meaningful statements. Avoid both excessive verbosity and artificially truncated bullet points.
- Use structured Markdown that is easy to read, understand, and revise.
- Aim for sufficient depth to confidently answer a typical 5-mark question, not textbook-level coverage.

## Working Rules

- Inspect relevant sources before writing.
- Maintain technical accuracy and consistent notation.
- Prefer existing repository materials over external references.
- Do not invent PYQ references, teacher instructions, or unsupported claims.
- Avoid unnecessary documentation, planning files, and duplicated content.
- Keep changes focused on the assigned task. Do not modify unrelated files.
- Verify coverage and correctness before completing each task.
