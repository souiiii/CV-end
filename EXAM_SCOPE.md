# Computer Vision End-Semester: Proposed Exam Scope

**Status: awaiting approval. No study notes have been written.**

## 1. Authority and confirmed boundaries

The older [teacher message](Syllabus/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-11-05%20at%203.38.29%20PM(1).jpeg) specifies four groups:

- **Image processing and video analysis:** frequency-domain analysis, time-domain analysis, image basics.
- **Object detection and tracking:** detection, tracking, and convolutional approaches.
- **Camera calibration:** vanishing points/lines and their uses, affine transformations, camera-parameter computation/estimation, calibration fundamentals.
- **Deep learning:** CNNs and generative models.

The [newer teacher message](Syllabus/SCR-20261113-rjac.png) explicitly says **Vision Transformers and RNNs will not come**. This overrides the older inclusion. Do not prepare transformer/attention or recurrent-network material as substitutes for these excluded topics.

The older message says **11 questions, 5 marks each**, with a **3/2/3/3** split. These are historical instructions, not a verified current paper pattern. The newer message supplies no replacement count or distribution. Prepare at roughly 5-mark explanatory/numerical depth without assuming unchanged section weights.

**Classification rule:** **Required** means a confirmed heading or the bounded breakdown proposed below; the teacher did not individually name every subtopic. **Minimal prerequisite** means only enough supporting material to understand a required topic. **Uncertain** means not authorized for full notes until its boundary is resolved. **Excluded** means omit from this preparation plan; distinguish explicit teacher exclusions from other material omitted because it falls outside the confirmed scope. Neither syllabus inclusion nor PYQ repetition establishes exam inclusion.

## 6. Preparation order and priority

All four teacher-specified groups have **required priority**. No ranking here predicts question counts, and no PYQ frequency is used as a probability estimate.

1. **Resolve the source gaps first:** clarify time-domain meaning, obtain/identify frequency-analysis and detection/tracking teaching material, and settle GAN-only versus broader generative coverage. Keep unresolved items visible rather than deleting them.
2. **Image basics and shared tools:** I1, then only the kernel/matrix prerequisites needed below. Study I2 once its missing teaching coverage is supplied or a bounded replacement is approved. Add I3 only after its meaning is settled.
3. **Camera block:** C2 and pinhole/frame basics, then C1, C3 and C4; revisit vanishing-point uses after parameter estimation. This block has especially explicit teacher subtopics and substantial supplied teaching material, even though it has few direct PYQs.
4. **CNN block:** N1–N3, prioritising layer diagrams, dimension/parameter calculations and learned-filter explanations. Then D1–D2 using the confirmed detection/tracking material; CNN knowledge is their prerequisite.
5. **Generative block:** G1–G2, with GAN data flow, objective and update sequence. Broader models enter only if the ambiguity is resolved in their favour. Finish with the matched PYQ portions in §3, adapting longer questions to concise 5-mark answers.
